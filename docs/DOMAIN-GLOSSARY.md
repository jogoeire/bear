# BEAR – Domain Glossary

## Roles

  - *PrimaryCaregiver*
    - Usually leads care coordination.
    - Can manage meds, messages, events.
    - Is the admin
  - *Caregiver*
    - Contributes messages, logs meds, participates in chat.
  - *CareRecipient*
    - The person receiving care.
    - Example: an elderly parent, a sick child, a partner with chronic illness.

- **Other role concepts**

- *CareCircle*
  - A group of people connected around one or more Care Recipients.
  - Includes:
    - Family members
    - Primary caregivers (professional or informal)
  - Each user has a **role** in each Care Circle.
- *User*
  - A person who logs into BEAR.
  - Can belong to multiple Care Circles.
  - Roles are per-circle.


## Communication Objects

- **Message**
  - A group of one or more messages about a theme
  - Authored by a User.
  - May include:
    - Text
    - Reactions
    - Attachments
